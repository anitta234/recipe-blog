Classic Chicken Fried Rice

Flavorful chicken fried rice that's quick and easy to prepare.

Ingredients

  • 2 cups cooked and cooled rice
  • 1 cup boneless chicken, cut into small pieces
  • 1 egg
  • 1 small carrot, finely chopped
  • 1/2 cup green peas
  • 1 small onion, finely chopped
  • 2 garlic cloves, minced
  • 1 tablespoon soy sauce
  • 1 teaspoon chilli sauce
  • 1/2 teaspoon black pepper powder
  • 2 tablespoons cooking oil
  • Salt to taste
  • 2 spring onions, chopped

Instructions

  1. Cook the rice and allow it to cool completely before preparing the fried rice.
  2. Heat 1 tablespoon of oil in a large pan or wok over medium-high heat.
  3. Add the chicken pieces and cook until they are fully cooked and lightly browned.
  4. Push the chicken to one side of the pan and add the remaining oil.
  5. Crack the egg into the pan and scramble it until cooked.
  6. Add the onion and garlic and stir-fry for about 1 minute.
  7. Add the carrot and green peas. Cook for 2–3 minutes.
  8. Add the cooled rice and mix everything together.
  9. Add soy sauce, chilli sauce, black pepper, and salt. Toss well.
  10. Stir-fry everything on high heat for another 2–3 minutes.
  11. Garnish with chopped spring onions.
  12. Serve hot and enjoy your chicken fried rice.
